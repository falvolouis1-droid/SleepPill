A GENTLER NIGHT STARTS HERE

Sleep tips.

Small habits can help make space for more restful nights.

  1. Consistent sleep schedule (waking and sleeping at the exact same time daily)
  2. Complete bedroom darkness using blackout curtains or an eye mask
  3. Cool ambient room temperature (around 18°C or 65°F)
  4. Complete cessation of caffeine at least 8 to 10 hours before bed
  5. No heavy meals or alcohol within 3 hours of sleeping
  6. Strict screen ban (no phones, tablets, or computers) for 60 minutes prior to rest
  7. Morning sunlight exposure within 30 minutes of waking to anchor the circadian rhythm
  8. White noise machine or earplugs to eliminate auditory sleep disruptions
  9. Magnesium glycinate supplementation taken roughly 30 to 60 minutes before bedtime
  10. Wind-down routine (such as reading fiction or light stretching) to signal the nervous system for rest
